The Heritage

More Than Just a Beach. It's a Lifestyle.

Originally a traditional fishing village, Arambol found fame in the 1960s with the arrival of the hippie trail. Today, it proudly maintains its strong bohemian culture, offering a stark, tranquil contrast to Goa's heavily commercialized hubs.

Here, the day blends seamlessly into night. From walk-in yoga retreats and vegan cafes to spontaneous trance music and art workshops, Arambol is a global melting pot for alternative travelers seeking creative expression and spiritual connection.

A Perfect Day in Arambol

Not sure how to spend your time? Here is a suggested daily rhythm to help you balance relaxation, adventure, and cultural immersion. Download our digital pamphlet for offline use.

Morning Yoga

7:00 AM

Start your day with a drop-in Ashtanga or Hatha class at one of the many beachside shalas.

Lake & Jungle Trek

10:30 AM

Hike to Sweet Water Lake for a mud bath, then venture into the jungle to find the Banyan Tree.

2:00 PM

Take to the skies with tandem paragliding off the northern cliffs or hit the waves for a surf lesson.

5:30 PM

Gather at the main beach. Shop at the flea market, watch fire dancers, and dance to the rhythm of the drums.

Logistics

Getting to Arambol

Navigating to North Goa is straightforward. Here are the main transit hubs and how to complete your journey.

Dabolim Airport

The primary air gateway, located ~59 km away. Operates 24/7 with domestic and international terminals.

How to reach beach: Take an airport shuttle/taxi to Panaji, then transfer to a local northbound bus or taxi.

Pernem Station

The closest rail access point, located just 17 km from the coast. Ideal for domestic backpackers.

How to reach beach: Taxis and local rickshaws are readily available right outside the station for a short, direct ride.

Panaji Hub

The state capital and central hub where most major bus routes from across India converge.

How to reach beach: Direct local buses leave frequently from the main stand, taking about 1.5 to 2 hours.
